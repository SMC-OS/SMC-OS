## Privacy and data processing

- Review lawful-basis wording, controller/processor allocation and UK GDPR terminology.
- Review the DPA, customer instructions, assistance, audits, security incident commitments and international-transfer wording.
- Confirm actual subprocessors, service locations, transfer mechanisms and change-notice process.
- Set defensible retention periods for billing, security/audit, suppression, copyright evidence and backups.
- Confirm data-subject rights workflow and ICO-facing wording without inventing registration details.

## Commercial terms

- Review liability caps, exclusions, disclaimers, indemnity, suspension and service-change clauses.
- Confirm B2B/consumer-law boundary, eligibility and authority-to-contract wording.
- Confirm recurring billing, renewal, cancellation, customer-portal and any later refund policy.
- Review governing law/jurisdiction, electronic notices and policy-update process.

## Content and platform risk

- Review copyright-report, response and counter-notice process; no US DMCA-agent registration is claimed.
- Review AI provider disclosure, outputs disclaimer, customer authorisation and data-minimisation wording.
- Confirm all public legal links, effective dates and policy-version maintenance before production release.
