# GeoCore Legal Review Checklist

This is an internal pre-publication review aid. It is not customer-facing legal advice.

## Operator, trading identity and tax

- Owner decision (2 November 2026): GeoCore is currently operated by **Mohammad Al Kawarit, trading as GeoCore**, as a UK **sole trader**. There is no incorporated GeoCore legal entity; do not describe the operator as GEOCORE OS LTD or show a Companies House number. Incorporating in the future is a separate decision.
- Confirm and publish a legitimate UK address for business correspondence/service of documents before releasing these public legal documents or accepting paid subscriptions. Do not infer the address from previous drafts or expose a personal residential address without explicit owner approval.
- Confirm whether any real trading has begun. Although the owner considers the product pre-trading, the live Stripe account was observed to contain an active and a cancelled subscription; establish whether these are internal tests or actual customer transactions and reconcile any tax/accounting consequences.
- Check HMRC Self Assessment sole-trader obligations if trading receipts require it; do not claim completed HMRC registration. Confirm VAT status, any VAT number and price/tax presentation only with evidence.
- Before taking paid orders, verify the legal operator identity in the live Stripe account, business disclosures, billing receipts, invoices, subscription cancellation and support pages are consistent. Never alter existing subscriptions as part of an identity-only change.
- If incorporating later, execute a separately reviewed transfer of contracts, privacy-controller notices and payment account details.

## Privacy and data processing

- Review lawful-basis wording, controller/processor allocation and UK GDPR terminology.
- Review the DPA, customer instructions, assistance, audits, security incident commitments and international-transfer wording.
- Confirm actual subprocessors, service locations, transfer mechanisms and change-notice process.
- Set defensible retention periods for billing, security/audit, suppression, copyright evidence and backups.
- Confirm data-subject rights workflow and ICO-facing wording without inventing registration details.

## Commercial terms

- Review liability caps, exclusions, disclaimers, indemnity, suspension and service-change clauses.
- Confirm B2B/consumer-law boundary, eligibility and authority-to-contract wording.
- Confirm recurring billing, renewal, cancellation, customer-portal and any later refund policy.
- Review governing law/jurisdiction, electronic notices and policy-update process.

## Content and platform risk

- Review copyright-report, response and counter-notice process; no US DMCA-agent registration is claimed.
- Review AI provider disclosure, outputs disclaimer, customer authorisation and data-minimisation wording.
- Confirm all public legal links, effective dates and policy-version maintenance before production release.
